How the badge decides

It scores the shape of the last 63 daily prices: how steady the move was, how straight, how efficient, and where the latest price sits in its range. The same prices always give the same badge.

Bullish

Prices climbed, and the climb is too large and too steady to be random noise.

Bearish

The same test, going down.

Neutral

No clear direction. This is the most common result, on purpose: the test is strict.

The badge describes what prices already did. It is not a forecast and not investment advice, and every card prints a short line saying so.

Questions

Does it include price data?

No. You supply the prices, so you choose the source: a paid data provider, your broker's export, or your own published prices. Use prices you have the right to show.

What kind of prices does it need?

One price per trading day (daily closes), oldest or newest first. It judges the most recent 63 by default, about three months.

Will it work on my site?

It works on any page where you can add your own HTML and a script tag, in current Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, on desktop and mobile.

Does the badge predict prices?

No. It labels the shape of past prices. It is not a forecast and not investment advice.

How many websites can I use it on?

As many as you own or run, plus the sites you build for clients. You may not resell or share the widget itself.

What if a price file fails to load?

The card says the prices could not be loaded. A failed load is never shown as a Neutral reading.
